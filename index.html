FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Everything people ask before their first virtual visit. Still curious? Our care team replies in under an hour.

No. PulseCare works in any modern browser, and you can switch to phone or encrypted SMS chat mid-visit if your connection drops. Most people use a phone — the app or a plain video link both work.

Every clinician completes primary-source credentialing, holds an unrestricted license in at least one state served by the platform, and is re-verified every two years. You can see license numbers, specialties and board certifications on each doctor's profile before you book.

Primary care visits are $29, mental health $49 and specialist consults $79. We check your insurance before the visit and show your exact out-of-pocket cost at checkout. Members on the Care+ plan pay nothing for the first three visits each month.

Yes, where clinically appropriate and permitted by your state's regulations. Prescriptions are sent electronically to any of 42,000 partner pharmacies, usually ready within 24 hours, or to your preferred local pharmacy.

Refill requests sit in your app with a one-tap approve button, and we check every refill against your current medication list for interactions before it ships. Automatic refill schedules are available for ongoing prescriptions.

PulseCare is HIPAA compliant with end-to-end encryption in transit and at rest, data residency in the US/EU, granular sharing controls, and a documented zero-data-retention policy on video streams. Video is never recorded unless both you and your doctor explicitly enable it.

Telemedicine covers the large majority of everyday concerns. For chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, stroke symptoms or major trauma, call emergency services first — our app will route you to the right level of care immediately.

With one tap you can share any visit summary, lab result or prescription with your primary care provider, and we support direct record exchange with 78% of US health systems. Your data is never shared without explicit consent.
