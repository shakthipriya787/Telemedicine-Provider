Our Mission

Make great medicine feel effortless

Healthcare fails people on the boring days: the ones where you can't get a ride, can't leave work, can't afford a missed shift, or simply can't find a slot that fits. Our mission is to remove every one of those excuses between a concern and a clinician.

That means building software that a 74-year-old can use without a manual, that a nurse can trust in an emergency, and that a health system can plug into without a nine-month integration project.

  • Clinical safety is non-negotiable

    Every protocol is reviewed by a medical director, and any visit can be escalated to in-person care in one click.

  • Transparent by default

    Price before booking, license numbers on every profile, notes in your hands immediately.

  • Designed for the hardest days

    Low-bandwidth mode, SMS fallback, screen-reader first, and 22 languages supported.

From a break room to a national network

Ten years of pivots, hard calls and the moments that shaped the company.

2016

The 1am phone call

Two ER nurses and a software engineer sketch a video-call prototype on a whiteboard during a night shift. First test: a single tablet and a very tired patient.

2017

License by license

We start with 14 clinicians in one state. Every state expansion takes four months of legal work â€” a pace we still keep, because it's the right pace.

2019

Records, not silos

2021

Care at home arrives

At-home diagnostics launch nationally. Sample-to-interpretation time drops from nine days to under 36 hours.
