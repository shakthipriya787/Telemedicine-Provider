Prepare for your visit

Tap a topic for what to do beforehand

Test your camera and microphone in the app 10 minutes early, charge your device, and use a private, well-lit space. Have your medication list and latest lab results open on screen.

Photo ID, your insurance card, a written list of questions, and any home readings you have logged. Arrive 15 minutes early to complete check-in.

Move or cancel any visit free of charge up to 2 hours before the start time. Late cancellations and no-shows are billed at $25.

Video visits are covered in full by Care+. In-person visits are billed after your plan's $25 copay. Statements appear in your account within 3 days of the visit.
