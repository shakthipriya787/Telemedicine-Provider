Understanding your results

What each marker means and what to do about it

HbA1c reflects your average blood sugar over roughly three months. Yours is 5.4%, comfortably inside the 4.0–5.6% normal range and down from 5.9% in November — keep up the current routine.

At 24 ng/mL you are just under the 30 ng/mL threshold. Dr. Fischer has started you on 2,000 IU of vitamin D3 daily and rechecking in about three months. Most people correct this within 8–12 weeks.

Each row shows the range typical for a healthy adult of your age and sex. Sitting just inside a boundary is normal — trends matter more than any single reading, which is why we plot every marker over time.

A clinician reviews every result before it reaches you. Out-of-range values get a note, a plan, and often a prescription update. You will also get a message if anything needs your input.
